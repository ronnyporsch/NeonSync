<h1 align="center">NeonSync</h1>

<p align="center">
  <strong>Bring your screen and music to life.</strong><br>
  Ambient lighting for <strong>Home Assistant</strong> lights.
</p>

NeonSync syncs your smart lights with the colours on your screen, the music you're playing, or both. Turn your room into an extension of your screen or let your lights dance to the beat.

### ✨ Features

- **Screen sync** – Your lights mirror the colours around the edges of your screen.
- **Music sync** – Lights react to audio played in real time.
- **Three modes** – Screen, Screen + Audio, or Audio only.
- **3D room setup** – Place your lights around your screen for natural, spatial effects.
- **Easy light selection** – Choose individual lights or entire Home Assistant areas.
- **Multiplatform** – Supports Linux, Browser, and Android (Audio only)

### Requirements

- Home Assistant with color-capable lights

### Installation (Arch Linux)

```bash
cd packaging/arch
makepkg -si
```

### Getting started

1. Open **Settings** and enter your Home Assistant address and access token. (to get a Token: Home Assistant > Profile > Security > Long-Lived Access Tokens)
2. Go to **Lights** and select the lights you want to control.
3. Arrange your lights in the **Room** view.
4. Choose a sync mode and enjoy!
